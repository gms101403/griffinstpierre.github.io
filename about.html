About Me

Engineering at the intersection of people and machines.

I am a biomedical and mechanical engineer interested in designing physical systems that interact directly with the human body. My interests center on medical devices, biomechanics, assistive technology, prosthetics, and human-machine interfaces.

My Engineering Approach

I enjoy taking engineering problems from an initial concept through CAD development, prototyping, testing, and refinement. I am particularly interested in the point where digital designs have to become physical products and the practical constraints of manufacturing, assembly, and use begin to influence the design.

My project work has given me experience working across mechanical design, biomechanics, rapid prototyping, mechanical testing, sensors, and data analysis. I enjoy learning through building and using physical prototypes to uncover problems that are not always apparent in a digital model.

Biomedical + Mechanical Engineering

My background in both biomedical and mechanical engineering allows me to approach medical-device problems from both the human and mechanical perspectives. I am interested in understanding how a device interacts with the body while also considering the mechanisms, materials, manufacturing processes, and constraints required to make the device work reliably.

What I Want to Build

I am particularly interested in engineering roles involving medical devices, mechanical design, biomechanics, prosthetics, assistive technology, robotics, and product development.

Long term, I want to work on products that combine strong engineering fundamentals with meaningful improvements to how people move, interact with technology, and live their daily lives.

Outside the Lab

I enjoy staying active and have a particular interest in sprinting and biomechanics. I also enjoy pickleball, which has provided another opportunity to explore the relationship between human movement and engineering through both competition and academic research.
