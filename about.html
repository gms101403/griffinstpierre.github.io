About Me

Engineering at the intersection of people and machines.

I am a biomedical and mechanical engineer interested in designing devices and systems that interact with the human body My interests center on medical devices, biomechanics, assistive technology, prosthetics, and human-machine interfaces.

My Engineering Approach

I enjoy taking engineering problems from an initial concept through CAD development, prototyping, testing, and refinement. I am particularly interested in the point where digital designs become physical products and the practical constraints of manufacturing, assembly, and real-world use begin to influence the design.

Through my project work, I have gained experience across mechanical design, biomechanics, rapid prototyping, mechanical testing, sensors, and data analysis. I enjoy learning through building and using physical prototypes to uncover problems that are not always apparent in a digital model.

Biomedical + Mechanical Engineering

Having studied both biomedical and mechanical engineering, I approach medical-device problems from both the human and mechanical perspectives.

I am interested in understanding how a device interacts with the body while also considering the mechanisms, materials, manufacturing processes, and constraints that make the device work reliably.

What I Want to Build

I am particularly interested in working on medical devices, mechanical systems, biomechanics, prosthetics, assistive technology, robotics, and other products that interact with the human body.

Long term, I want to work on products that combine strong engineering fundamentals with meaningful improvements to how people move, interact with technology, and live their daily lives.

Outside the Lab

I enjoy staying active, with a particular interest in sprinting and biomechanics. I also enjoy pickleball, which has given me another way to explore the relationship between human movement and engineering through both competition and academic research.
